# Migration Guide

The HPP Migration Portal enables holders of legacy AERGO and AQT to seamlessly convert their assets into HPP, the unified token that powers the House Party Protocol ecosystem.

Migration ensures that your tokens are compatible with the HPP Mainnet, unlocking full access to features such as AI agent execution, governance participation, and ecosystem rewards.
